# Hvalrossen Helle: prompt til Gemini Pro-appen

Vedhæft to billeder som forlæg, så Helle passer til de andre figurer:

- `games/maskinen/billeder/pindsvin.png` (Pelle)
- `games/bogstaver/billeder/egern.png` (Egon)

Prompt:

> Paint a new character for a children's picture-book game, in exactly the same
> style as the two attached characters: warm gouache picture-book painting, soft
> textured brushwork, muted cosy colours, gentle light, a friendly face with small
> rosy cheeks.
>
> The character is Helle, a big, round, happy walrus. Warm brown-grey skin, two
> short white tusks, a bushy moustache, small kind dark eyes, and two front
> flippers. She lies on her belly, propped up on her flippers, looking at the
> viewer with a big gentle smile, as if she is about to hum a word.
>
> Full body, centred, nothing cut off. Plain white background, no shadow on the
> ground, no ice, no water, no other objects. Square 1:1. No text, no letters, no
> frame.

Gem billedet som `helle.png` og send det i chatten. Baggrunden skæres fra i
kode, og billedet lægges i et kvadrat ligesom de andre figurer.
